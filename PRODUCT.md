# SensorGuard

## Register

product

## Users

Industrial operations staff monitor a small fleet, identify abnormal sensor readings, and follow incident recovery. Hackathon presenters and judges need to understand that workflow quickly on a projected dashboard.

## Product Purpose

Show simulated industrial telemetry flowing through MongoDB time series, BullMQ incident processing and recovery, with Datadog observability. A successful demo clearly distinguishes incoming data, detected faults, simulated remediation and confirmed recovery.

## Brand Personality

Precise, composed, capable. Use a dark industrial operations interface, restrained cyan for selection and semantic warning/error colors. Vietnamese interface labels support the local hackathon presentation; retain technical product names.

## Anti-references

Avoid luxury display typography, decorative glass effects, constantly moving tickers and unsupported claims of system health. Do not present simulated root causes or recovery as real hardware automation.

## Design Principles

- Make the demo journey visible: observe, trigger, investigate, verify.
- Show measured status and freshness, including unavailable data.
- Prioritize machine identity, sensor values and incident progress.
- Keep the screen readable during projection and on smaller laptops.
- Keep actions predictable and provide visible feedback on failure.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, visible keyboard focus and semantic controls. Pair colors with text, respect reduced motion, and keep device selection available on narrow screens.
